# VERDANT — CMS CLIENT HANDOFF

This document is the client-facing content and media checklist for the current Verdant storefront.

## 1. What the client can edit today

The current CMS/admin implementation covers:

- Products: name, slug, type, category, sub-category, price, stock, size/pack, care/use label, description, featured flag, sort order, optional badge, primary image and up to 8 product images.
- Home: Hero, announcement strip, Collections, Featured Products heading, Our Story, Plant Care, Newsletter and Footer.
- Catalog structure: 10 top-level categories and 38 sub-categories.
- Editorial pages: published CMS pages such as Plant Care and Our Story.
- Navigation/site content: managed through the CMS tables used by the site.

Product images are uploaded from the Admin Products screen. The storefront accepts a primary image plus an image gallery; the current UI supports up to 8 images per product.

## 2. Product image rules — use these for every product

### Primary product image

- 1200 × 1200 px preferred.
- Square 1:1 composition.
- Product centered with enough breathing room around it.
- Plain, light or softly natural background.
- No text, price stickers, badges or watermarks inside the image.
- Use the actual species/item sold, not a category illustration.
- For plants: show the whole sellable plant, preferably in its actual nursery pot or the pot configuration the client intends to sell.
- For gardening supplies: show the exact item/pack being sold.

### Gallery images

Use up to 8 images where useful:
1. Front/hero view.
2. Side or alternate angle.
3. Detail/texture/foliage close-up.
4. Scale reference.
5. Pot/packaging view.
6. Lifestyle placement.
7. Care/detail shot where relevant.
8. Back/label/contents for supplies.

Do not upload repeated crops of the same photo.

## 8. Client content quality rules

Keep product names specific and consistent.

Use one naming style across the catalogue: `Monstera Deliciosa`, `Snake Plant`, `Jade Plant` rather than mixing title case, all caps and shorthand.

Descriptions should explain the actual product in one or two useful sentences. Avoid unsupported medical, air-purifying, safety or performance claims unless the business has verified them.

For plant care labels, use simple categories such as `Easy care` and `Medium care` consistently.

For supplies, use practical labels such as `Easy` only when they describe usage appropriately.

## 9. Launch checklist

Before publishing:

- Every active product has a real primary image.
- Every product image matches the exact product name.
- Every featured product is intentionally selected.
- Prices, sizes/packs and stock are correct.
- Category and sub-category assignments are correct.
- Product descriptions are final.
- Plant care labels are final.
- Home copy is final.
- Collection links work.
- Plant Care and Our Story pages are published.
- No placeholder, generic category SVG or unrelated stock image remains on a product tile.
- Uploaded images are clear, square, consistent and free of watermarks/text overlays.
